<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new job">
    In **Jobs**, create a new job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Give it a **Job title**, for example "Lawn cleanup — 12 Oak St."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the client">
    Pick the **Client** and enter the **Address** of the job site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Set the **Start date**, **Start time** and **End time** under **Schedule**. To make it repeat, see [Recurring jobs](/jobs/recurring-jobs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign your crew">
    Choose the **Assigned crew** or **Technician**, and set a **Priority**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Add each **Product / Service** with its price, or click **Add from price book** to reuse your saved services. The **Total** updates as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an on-site checklist">
    Under **On-site checklist**, add the tasks the crew must do, such as "Check filter" or "Photograph finished work." The crew marks each one done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes and save">
    Leave an internal note for yourself or a team member, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Won a quote? Turn it into a job in one click instead of retyping it. See [Quotes](/quotes/overview).
</Tip>
