The **Price book** holds "reusable services & items — auto-fill them on quotes and jobs." Find it with **Ctrl K** by searching for "Price book."

## Add an item

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Name / description** | "Weekly lawn mowing" |
| **Category** | "Lawn" |
| **Description (optional)** | What's included. |
| **Unit** | Per visit, per hour, per item. |
| **Price (USD)** | `89.00` |

If you haven't added anything yet you'll see "No items yet. Add your common services to reuse them on quotes & jobs."

## Use it

On a [job](/jobs/create-a-job) or a [quote](/quotes/overview), click **Add from price book** and pick the item. You can still change the price on that job or quote.

<Tip>
  Add your 10–20 most common services first. It makes every quote faster and keeps your pricing consistent across the team.
</Tip>
