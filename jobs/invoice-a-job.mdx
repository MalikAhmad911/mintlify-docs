When the work is done, invoice it straight from the job so nothing has to be retyped.

## Before you invoice

- The job must be linked to a contact. If not, you'll see "Link a contact to the job before invoicing."
- The job needs line items with a total. If not, you'll see "Add line items with a total before invoicing."

## Create the invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Open the finished job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create invoice">
    Revenue Sol creates the invoice from the job's line items and marks the job **Invoiced**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the payment link">
    Revenue Sol creates a payment link for the invoice. Send it to the customer so they can pay from their phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A job can only be invoiced once. If you try again you'll see "This job has already been invoiced."

## After they pay

The payment shows up in **Payments**, and if review requests are turned on in [AI Brain](/ai/ai-features), the customer is asked for a review.
