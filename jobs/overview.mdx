Open **Jobs** for "work orders + dispatch — schedule, assign crew, track, and invoice." Jobs are included on every plan.

## Job statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Booked for a date and time. |
| **En route** | The technician is on the way. |
| **In progress** | Work has started. |
| **Completed** | The work is done. |
| **Invoiced** | An invoice has been sent for the job. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. |

Use **Change job status** on a job to move it along.

## Find jobs

- **Search jobs, customers, addresses…** at the top.
- Filter by status (**All statuses**), technician (**All technicians**) and date (**Today**, **Next 7 days** or **Any date**).

## Views

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **List view** | Seeing every job with its client, schedule and total. |
| **Dispatch view** | Planning the day: see who's doing what and reassign jobs by dragging. See [Dispatch board](/jobs/dispatch-board). |
