Moving from another tool? Import your jobs from a spreadsheet.

## Prepare your file

Use these columns, in this order:

| Title | Client | Address | Date | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lawn cleanup | Jane Smith | 12 Oak St | 2026-06-20 | 250 |

- **Title** is required. Rows without a title are skipped.
- Write dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Don't put commas inside a value.

## Import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import jobs">
    In **Jobs**, click **Import jobs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste or upload your rows">
    Upload the CSV or paste the rows. A header row is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Revenue Sol shows how many jobs were imported and how many were skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>
