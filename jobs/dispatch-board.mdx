Switch **Jobs** to **Dispatch view** to plan the day.

## See the day

The board shows each technician's jobs for the day. Use **Previous day**, **Today** and **Next day** to move around. Days with nothing booked show **No jobs scheduled for this day.**

## Reassign by dragging

Each technician has a column. Drag a job into another technician's column (where it says **Drop jobs here**) to give them the job.

## Reschedule

To change a job's date or time, click **Reschedule** on the job.

## Route

Open **Route** for "today's route for the crew." Jobs without an address show **No address**, so add one to include them.

## Clock in and out

On the dispatch board, click **Clock in** when work starts on a job and **Clock out** when it ends. Revenue Sol records the time spent on the job.

<Tip>
  Ask technicians to clock in on arrival. It gives you real job times for future quotes.
</Tip>
