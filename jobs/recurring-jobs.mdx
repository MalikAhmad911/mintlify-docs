Recurring jobs are made for regular work like weekly lawn mowing, biweekly cleaning or monthly pool service.

## Make a job repeat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Create a job or open an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the repeat schedule">
    Under **Repeats**, choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Revenue Sol creates the upcoming visits on your schedule, each as its own job you can assign and track.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bill recurring work automatically

Charging the same amount every visit or every month? Pair recurring jobs with recurring invoices, which "auto-send invoices on a schedule — memberships, maintenance, lawn, cleaning." Find **Recurring invoices** under **Payments**.
