Revenue Sol sends and receives texts and calls through **your own phone provider**. Until you connect one, texts you write in the inbox aren't delivered and the AI receptionist can't answer calls.

## Choose your provider

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Twilio" icon="message-sms" href="/phone/connect-twilio">
    Best if you're starting fresh. You buy a number in Twilio and connect it in a few clicks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RingCentral" icon="phone-office" href="/phone/connect-ringcentral">
    Best if your business already uses RingCentral for its phones.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What works once a number is connected

| Feature | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Two-way texting from the inbox | Every plan |
| Calling from your browser | Every plan |
| [Missed-call follow-up](/phone/missed-call-text-back) (automatic text back) | Professional and up |
| 24/7 AI receptionist that answers calls | Professional and up |
| SMS campaigns | Professional and up |

## Who pays for calls and texts

Your phone provider bills you directly for numbers, calls and texts at their own rates. Revenue Sol doesn't add a markup.

## Setup checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a number">
    [Get a Twilio number](/phone/get-a-twilio-number), or use a number you already have in RingCentral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for business texting (US)">
    Texting customers in the US from a local number requires [A2P 10DLC registration](/phone/a2p-10dlc) with your provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it in Revenue Sol">
    Open **Settings → Connectors** and connect [Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio) or [RingCentral](/phone/connect-ringcentral).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Test Connection**, then send yourself a text from the inbox. See [Test and troubleshoot](/phone/troubleshooting).
  </Step>
</Steps>
