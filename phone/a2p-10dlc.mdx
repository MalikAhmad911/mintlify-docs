**A2P 10DLC** is the system US mobile carriers use to approve businesses that send texts from regular 10-digit local numbers. "A2P" means application-to-person: texts sent from software like Revenue Sol rather than from a personal phone.

## Why it matters

US carriers filter or block business texts from local numbers that aren't registered. If your texts aren't reaching customers, missing registration is one of the most common reasons.

## What you register

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand** | Your business: legal name, address, tax ID (EIN) and website. |
| **Campaign** | How you text customers, for example appointment reminders, quotes and customer replies, with sample messages. |

## How to register in Twilio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open A2P 10DLC in the Twilio Console">
    Sign in to Twilio and open the A2P 10DLC (regulatory compliance) section of the Console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your brand">
    Enter your business details exactly as they appear on your tax records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your campaign">
    Describe how you text customers and add sample messages, such as "Hi Maria, this is Reyes HVAC. Your technician arrives at 9am tomorrow. Reply STOP to opt out."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your number to the campaign">
    Attach the number you connected to Revenue Sol.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approval usually takes a few days. Twilio's guide walks through each field: [twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc).

## Texting rules to follow

- Only text people who gave you their number or asked you to contact them.
- Honor opt-outs. When a customer replies **STOP**, don't text them again.
- Identify your business in the first message of a conversation.

<Note>
  If you use RingCentral, register through RingCentral instead. Your RingCentral admin can see the status in the RingCentral admin portal.
</Note>
