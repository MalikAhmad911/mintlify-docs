Call customers right from Revenue Sol. The call goes out from your connected business number, so customers see the number they know.

<Note>
  Calling needs a connected [Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio) or [RingCentral](/phone/connect-ringcentral) number, and permission to use your microphone in the browser.
</Note>

## Start a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialpad">
    In **Calls**, click **New call** to open the **Dialpad**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number">
    Type it in **Enter a number**, or use **Paste number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call">
    Click **Call**. Allow microphone access if your browser asks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the call

The call appears in [Call history](/phone/calls) with its recording, transcript and AI summary when available.

<Tip>
  Use a headset for clearer audio and to avoid echo.
</Tip>
