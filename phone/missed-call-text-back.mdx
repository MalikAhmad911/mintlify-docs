When you can't pick up, Revenue Sol can text the caller right away so they don't call the next business on Google.

<Note>
  You need a connected [Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio) or [RingCentral](/phone/connect-ringcentral) number.
</Note>

## Turn on Missed-call follow-up

Available on **Professional** and up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Brain">
    Click **AI Brain** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Missed-call follow-up">
    Switch on **Missed-call follow-up**. It "texts back anyone whose call you missed."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check AI Autopilot is on">
    Missed-call follow-up runs as part of Autopilot. Make sure Autopilot is on at the top of **AI Brain**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When a call is missed, the AI texts the caller and the conversation continues in your **Inbox**.

<Warning>
  Missed-call follow-up only runs when **Autopilot** is on. If you've set business hours for Autopilot, it only texts back calls missed during those hours. If Autopilot is set to ask for approval, the text waits in the approval queue in **AI Brain** until you approve it.
</Warning>

## Build your own text with Automations

On **Growth**, you can write the exact message yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automations">
    Click **Automations** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Missed Call → Auto SMS template">
    Choose **Missed Call -> Auto SMS** ("Send a quick text when a customer call is missed").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the message">
    The template starts with: "Hi {first_name}, sorry we missed your call. Reply here and we'll help you shortly." Change it to suit your business, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Mention your business name in the text so the caller knows who's replying.
</Tip>
