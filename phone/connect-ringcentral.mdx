If your business already runs on RingCentral, connect it to Revenue Sol and keep your current number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and open **RingCentral**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Sign in to RingCentral and approve access for your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your business number">
    Pick the **Business number** customers text and call. Revenue Sol shows your **Account**, **Extension** and **Current extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Use the test option on the RingCentral panel, then send yourself a text from the **Inbox**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browser calling

Once RingCentral is connected, browser calling is set up automatically. You can call customers from Revenue Sol without picking up your desk phone.

## If something stops working

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No active numbers found** | Make sure the extension you signed in with has an SMS-enabled number in RingCentral, then refresh numbers. |
| A webhook warning | Use the repair option on the RingCentral panel to restore text and call routing. |
| An error after changing your RingCentral password | Click **Reconnect** and sign in again. |

<Note>
  RingCentral bills you directly for your numbers, calls and texts.
</Note>
