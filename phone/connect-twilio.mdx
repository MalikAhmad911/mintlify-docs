Connecting Twilio lets Revenue Sol send and receive texts and calls on your Twilio number, text back missed calls and run the AI receptionist.

<Note>
  You need a Twilio account with at least one phone number. Don't have one yet? See [Get a Twilio number](/phone/get-a-twilio-number).
</Note>

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    In Revenue Sol, go to **Settings → Connectors** and open **Twilio**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Twilio">
    Click **Connect Twilio**. You're taken to Twilio to sign in and approve access for your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access in Twilio">
    Sign in to Twilio and approve the connection. You come back to Revenue Sol and see **Twilio connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default number">
    Your Twilio numbers sync automatically. Under **Choose a default number**, pick the number customers will text and call.

    When you save it, Revenue Sol sets up the number's text and call webhooks for you and shows **Default Twilio number saved and webhooks configured**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection**. Then send yourself a text from the **Inbox** to make sure it arrives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see on the Twilio page

| Item | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Integration status** | Whether Twilio is connected. |
| **Connected account** | The Twilio account linked to this workspace (partly hidden for security). |
| **Default Twilio number** | The number used for texts and calls. |
| **Numbers** | Every number in your Twilio account, with SMS, MMS and Voice capabilities. |
| **Webhook status** | Whether text and call routing is set up on the default number. |
| **Last sync** | When the number list was last refreshed. |

## Added a number in Twilio later?

Click **Refresh Numbers** on the Twilio page so the new number shows up, then choose it as your default if you want to use it.

## Reconnecting

If Twilio shows an error or you changed your Twilio login, click **Reconnect** to refresh the connection. Your settings and history stay as they are.
