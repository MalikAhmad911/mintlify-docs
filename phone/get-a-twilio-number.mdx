Revenue Sol uses your own Twilio account, so you own the number and Twilio bills you directly for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Twilio account">
    Sign up at [twilio.com](https://www.twilio.com) and verify your email and phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade from a trial account">
    Twilio trial accounts can only text and call numbers you've verified, and add a trial notice to messages. Add billing in Twilio to upgrade before using the number with customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a number">
    In the Twilio Console, open the phone numbers section and buy a local number in your area code. Make sure it supports **SMS** and **Voice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for texting">
    To text US customers from a local number, complete [A2P 10DLC registration](/phone/a2p-10dlc) in Twilio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it to Revenue Sol">
    Follow [Connect Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already have a business number with another carrier? Twilio can port it so you keep the number customers know. Check Twilio's porting guide before you start.
</Tip>

## Which number should you use?

- A **local number** in your service area looks familiar to customers and is the usual choice for service businesses.
- Use one number for both texts and calls so customers can reply to the number that called them.
