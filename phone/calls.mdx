Open **Calls** in the sidebar to see every call to and from your business number: "History, voicemail, transcripts and AI summaries."

## Find a call

- Use **Search calls by name or number...** at the top.
- Click **Refresh calls** to load the latest.
- Click **Sync calls** ("Sync provider call history") to pull in calls from Twilio or RingCentral that haven't shown up yet.

## Call details

Pick a call to see its **Details**:

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Business line** | Which of your numbers the call came in on. |
| **Recording** | Play the call recording, if one was made. |
| **View transcript** | The full text of the call. |
| **AI summary** | A short summary of what the call was about. Click **Regenerate** to create it again. |

## Save a caller as a contact

If the caller isn't in your contacts yet, click **Save contact**, enter the **Customer name**, and save. Future calls and texts from that number are linked to the contact.

## From a call to a conversation

From the call details you can **Text** the caller back, or open their profile in the CRM.
