## Use a different number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your provider">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and open **Twilio** or **RingCentral**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh your numbers">
    If the new number was just added, click **Refresh Numbers** (Twilio) so it appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose it as the default">
    Select it as the **Default number** and save. Revenue Sol sets up its text and call routing for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Tell regular customers before you switch numbers, so their replies go to the right place.
</Tip>

## Disconnect Twilio

On the Twilio page, click **Disconnect** and confirm. The Twilio connection and the synced number list are removed from your workspace. Your contacts and past conversations stay in Revenue Sol.

After you disconnect, texts and calls stop until you connect a provider again.

## Move from one provider to another

Connect the new provider first, choose its default number, test it, then disconnect the old one. That way customers never hit a dead number.
