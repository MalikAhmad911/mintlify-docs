## Test your setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    In **Settings → Connectors**, open your provider and click **Test Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Text yourself">
    Add yourself as a contact and send a text from the **Inbox**. Reply from your phone to check incoming texts too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call yourself">
    Call your business number from your cell. The call should appear in **Calls**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;SMS could not be delivered: Connect Twilio before sending SMS&quot;">
    No phone number is connected yet, so the text stayed in Revenue Sol. [Connect Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio) or [RingCentral](/phone/connect-ringcentral), then send it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Texts send but customers don't get them">
    The most common cause is missing [A2P 10DLC registration](/phone/a2p-10dlc). US carriers filter business texts from unregistered local numbers. Also check the customer hasn't replied **STOP**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Webhook status shows a problem">
    Open your provider in **Settings → Connectors**. For Twilio, choose your default number again so the webhooks are set up. For RingCentral, use the repair option on the panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Incoming texts or calls don't show up">
    Make sure the number customers use is the **default number** in Revenue Sol. Then click **Refresh Numbers** (Twilio) and **Sync calls** in **Calls**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Twilio trial account">
    Twilio trial accounts only reach numbers you've verified in Twilio. Upgrade your Twilio account to text and call customers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calls from the browser have no audio">
    Allow microphone access for app.revenuesol.com in your browser settings, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missed calls aren't getting a text back">
    Check that **Autopilot** and **Missed-call follow-up** are both on in **AI Brain**, and whether Autopilot business hours are limiting when it runs. See [Missed-call text back](/phone/missed-call-text-back).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
