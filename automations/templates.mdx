Open **Automations → Templates** and pick one. Each opens as a draft you can edit before turning it on.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="After Payment -> Review Request">
    Asks the customer for a review once they've paid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missed Call -> Auto SMS">
    "Send a quick text when a customer call is missed." It starts with: "Hi {first_name}, sorry we missed your call. Reply here and we'll help you shortly."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No Reply 24hr -> Follow Up">
    "Follow up with leads who do not respond."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Click it in **Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it">
    Change the message, conditions or timing. See [Create an automation](/automations/create-an-automation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Switch on **Automation active** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>
