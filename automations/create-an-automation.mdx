"Build a business-friendly rule without writing technical prompts." In **Automations**, click **Create Automation**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Give it an **Automation Name**, such as "Text new leads."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger">
    Pick when it runs. See [Triggers and actions](/automations/triggers-and-actions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions (optional)">
    Narrow it down with **Contact Stage**, **Contact Tag** or **Payment amount above**, and tick **Business hours only** to keep it inside your working hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a delay (optional)">
    Use **Delay before running** to wait before acting, for example 10 minutes after a new lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Pick what it does, such as **Send SMS**, and write the **Message Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let AI write it (optional)">
    Turn on **Use AI Agent** to "let an Infinite Agent write or adapt the message," and choose an **AI Behavior**, such as **Write short reply** or **Escalate if unsure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and turn it on">
    Review the **Validation checklist**, switch on **Automation active** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with one simple automation, watch it in **History** for a few days, then add more.
</Tip>

## Placeholders in messages

Use these in a **Message Template**. Revenue Sol fills them in for each contact:

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{first_name}` | The contact's first name ("there" if unknown) |
| `{last_name}` | The contact's last name |
| `{business_name}` | Your business name |
| `{phone}` | Your business phone number |
| `{amount}` | The payment amount, for payment triggers |
| `{invoice_link}` | The payment link, for payment triggers |
| `{booking_link}` | Your online booking page |
| `{review_link}` | Your Google review link, when one is saved |

For example: "Hi {first_name}, thanks for choosing {business_name}! Book your next visit here: {booking_link}"

## Turn off or delete

Switch off **Active** to pause an automation. Use **Delete** to remove it.
