**Automations** help you "save hours a week — pick a recipe, we handle the plumbing." Each automation is a rule: **when** something happens, **do** something. Automations are included on **Growth** and up.

## How a rule works

| Part | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger**: when it runs | When a call is missed |
| **Conditions** (optional): only if | Contact stage is Lead, during business hours |
| **Action**: what it does | Send SMS: "Sorry we missed your call…" |

## Your automations page

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Your automations and whether each is on. |
| **Templates** | Ready-made recipes to start from. See [Templates](/automations/templates). |
| **History** | **Recent automation activity**: what ran, when and whether it worked. |

At the top you'll see **Runs · 30d**, **Success rate** and **Time saved**.
