The **Revenue Sol — Forms & Chat** plugin adds your lead form and the website chat widget to any WordPress site. Every form submission becomes a lead in Revenue Sol, and chat messages land in your [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the plugin">
    In Revenue Sol, go to **Settings → Connected Apps**. On the **Website** card, under **WordPress / Elementor plugin**, click **Download WordPress plugin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it to WordPress">
    In WordPress, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, upload the file, click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your form link">
    In WordPress, open **Settings → Revenue Sol**. In Revenue Sol, copy the last part of **Your public link** from the **Request Service Form** section of the **Website** card (for example `acme-plumbing`) and paste it into **Your form link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on chat (optional)">
    Paste the **API Key** from the **Website** card into **Website chat widget — API key**. The chat bubble then appears on every page. Leave it blank to keep chat off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    A live preview appears at the bottom of the settings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add the form to a page

| Editor | How |
| --- | --- |
| Any page builder | Add the shortcode `[revenuesol_form]` |
| Block editor | Add a **Shortcode** block and paste `[revenuesol_form]` |
| Elementor | Drag a **Shortcode** widget and paste `[revenuesol_form]` |

## Replace Contact Form 7

Turn on **Replace Contact Form 7** in the plugin settings, and every existing `[contact-form-7]` form on your site is swapped for your Revenue Sol form, with no page edits needed.

Other form plugins, such as WPForms, Gravity Forms or native Elementor forms, aren't replaced automatically. Add `[revenuesol_form]` where you want the form instead.

## Options

- **Form height (px)**: how tall the form is on the page.
- Shortcode attributes: `[revenuesol_form slug="acme-plumbing" height="820"]` shows a specific form at a set height.

<Note>
  The form is hosted and protected by Revenue Sol, with spam protection and rate limits.
</Note>
