Your **Request Service Form** is a ready-made intake form customers use to ask for a visit or a quote. Every submission lands in your Leads inbox.

## Find your link

Go to **Settings → Connected Apps** and find **Request Service Form** on the **Website** card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your link">
    Under **Your public link**, type the last part of the address, such as `acme-plumbing`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. This is the URL your customers see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Click **Preview** to open the form the way customers see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share it

Click **Copy Link** to copy the **Shareable link**, then:


- Link to it from your website's "Request service" or "Get a quote" button.
- Add it to your Google Business Profile, social bios and email signature.

## Put it on your website

- **WordPress**: use the [WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress-plugin).
- **Any other website builder**: click **Copy Embed** and paste the **Embed (iframe)** code into an HTML or embed block where you want the form.

## What happens next

Each submission lands in your Leads inbox, so you can follow up from your [Inbox](/inbox/overview) and track the customer in [Contacts](/contacts/overview).
