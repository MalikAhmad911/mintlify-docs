Go to **Settings → Connected Apps** and click **Open hub** to reach the **Connectors** hub, where you connect every app from one place. Each connector shows **Connected**, **Not connected** or **Reconnect needed**, so you can spot anything that needs attention.

The **Website** card (your API key, chat widget, reviews widget, WordPress plugin and request form) sits on the **Connected Apps** tab itself, below the hub link.

| Connector | What it's for | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Twilio** | Texts, calls and missed-call text back | [Connect Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio) |
| **RingCentral** | Texts and calls on your RingCentral number | [Connect RingCentral](/phone/connect-ringcentral) |
| **Retell AI** | The 24/7 AI receptionist | [Connect Retell](/ai/connect-retell) |
| **Google** | Google reviews, Search Console and Analytics 4 | [Connect Google reviews](/reviews/connect-google), [Web analytics](/analytics/web-analytics) |
| **Meta** | WhatsApp and Instagram messages | [WhatsApp and Instagram](/inbox/whatsapp-instagram) |
| **QuickBooks Online** | Accounting sync | [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks) |
| **Website** | Chat widget, reviews widget, request form and WordPress plugin | [Website chat widget](/inbox/website-chat-widget), [Request service form](/integrations/request-form), [WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress-plugin) |

## If a connector needs attention

A connector that's lost access shows **Reconnect needed**. Open it and connect again to sign back in.
