Keep your customer, invoice and payment information accurate between Revenue Sol and QuickBooks Online automatically, with no double entry. QuickBooks Online is included in your plan.

## What syncs

- **Customers, invoices and payments** sync to QuickBooks Online.
- **Payments recorded in Revenue Sol are matched against QuickBooks invoices.**
- **Inventory**: when you use a product in a job, its quantity is updated in QuickBooks Online.

## Connect QuickBooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open QuickBooks Online">
    Open the **Connectors** hub (**Settings → Connected Apps → Open hub**) and choose **QuickBooks Online**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click connect and sign in to QuickBooks with an admin account for your company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Approve the connection. You come back to Revenue Sol and the status changes to **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnecting

QuickBooks connections expire from time to time. If you see **Reconnect needed — your QuickBooks token expired.**, click reconnect and sign in again.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** and confirm. New invoices and payments stop syncing until you reconnect. Data already in QuickBooks stays there.
