Click a contact to open their profile.

## Sections

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **At a glance** | A quick summary of the contact. |
| **Contact info** | Name, phone, email and company. |
| **Activity** | Conversations, calls, payments and other activity with this contact. Shows **No activity yet** for new contacts. |
| **Internal notes** | Notes only your team sees. |
| **Tags** | The tags on this contact. |

## Quick actions

From the profile you can **Send SMS**, **Request payment** or **Send review request** without going to another page.

## Keep it up to date

- Move the contact to the right **Stage** as the job progresses. See [Stages and tags](/contacts/stages-and-tags).
- Write notes your team will need on the next visit, such as equipment details or access instructions.
