## Stages

Every contact has one stage: **Lead**, **Prospect**, **Customer**, **VIP** or **Lost**. Change it on the contact, or for many leads at once with **Change stage** on the **Sales** page. See [Contacts overview](/contacts/overview#contact-stages) for what each stage means.

## Tags

Tags are free-form labels. A contact can have as many as you need.

Ideas that work well for service businesses:

| Tag | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| `Maintenance plan` | Find everyone due for a seasonal visit. |
| `Commercial` | Separate business customers from homeowners. |
| `Hot Lead` | Flag leads to call back today. |
| `Warranty` | Track jobs still under warranty. |

## Add or remove tags in bulk

On the **Sales** page, select several leads and choose **Add tag** or **Remove tag**, then enter the tag name. See [Sales pipeline](/contacts/sales-pipeline#bulk-actions).

## Use tags

- **Filter by tag** in Contacts to see one group.
- Target a group with an SMS campaign.
