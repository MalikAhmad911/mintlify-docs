Open **Contacts** to "manage leads, customers, VIPs, and lost contacts in one place." Contacts are unlimited on every plan.

## At the top

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Total contacts** | Everyone in your workspace. |
| **Customers** | Contacts at the Customer stage. |
| **VIP contacts** | Contacts at the VIP stage. |
| **New this month** | Contacts added this month. |

## Contact stages

| Stage | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead** | Someone who reached out or was added but hasn't bought yet. |
| **Prospect** | A lead you're actively quoting or working. |
| **Customer** | Someone who has paid you. |
| **VIP** | Your best repeat customers. |
| **Lost** | A lead who chose someone else or went quiet. |

## Find contacts

- Use **Search contacts...** to find someone.
- **Filter by tag** to see one group, such as "Maintenance plan."
- **Sort contacts** by **Recent**, **Name** or **Lead score**.

## Act on a contact

From the list, open **Actions** on a contact to **Send SMS**, **Request payment** or **Send review request**, or click **View contact** to open the full [contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile).
