Open **Sales** in the sidebar to "track and manage your sales pipeline from first touch to won."

## Pipeline stages

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Just came in. |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out. |
| **Qualified** | A real job you can win. |
| **Won** | They hired you. |
| **Lost** | They didn't. |

**Pipeline overview** shows how many leads are at each stage.

## What each lead shows

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** and **Contact** | Who they are and how to reach them. |
| **Status** | Their pipeline stage. |
| **Source** | Where the lead came from. |
| **Score** | Their lead score: how engaged and likely to book they are. |
| **CLV** | Lifetime value: what they've spent with you so far. |
| **Created** | When the lead was added. |

Use **Search leads...** and **Sort leads** to find who to call next.

## Add a lead

Click **Add new lead** and fill in **Full name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Company**, **Source** and **Notes**.

## Bulk actions

Select several leads to act on them together:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Change stage** | Move them all to a new stage. |
| **Add tag** / **Remove tag** | Tag or untag them. |
| **SMS campaign** | Create an SMS campaign to the selected leads. |
| **Review request** | Send review requests. |
| **Export CSV** | Download them as a spreadsheet. |

<Note>
  SMS campaigns go only to leads who can be texted. Anyone who opted out is left out. See [Opt-outs](/inbox/opt-outs).
</Note>
