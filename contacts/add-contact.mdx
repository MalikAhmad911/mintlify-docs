---
title: "Add a contact by hand"
sidebarTitle: "Add a contact"
description: "Add a customer or lead to Revenue Sol: full name, phone, email, company, source, stage, tags and notes. Takes under a minute."
keywords: ["add contact CRM", "add customer", "new lead", "create contact"]
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Contacts**, click **Add contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Basic information">
    Enter the **Full name** (for example "Jane Smith"), **Phone** with the country code (for example `+1 555 123 4567`) and **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organization">
    Add the **Company** if they're a business customer, such as a property manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classification">
    Choose the **Stage**, where they came from in **Source** (for example "Website, referral, ad...") and any **Tags**, separated by commas ("Hot Lead, VIP").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notes">
    Add useful context about this contact, such as gate codes or the best time to call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The contact appears in your list right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always add a phone number. It's what links texts and calls to the right contact.
</Tip>

You can also add someone from the **Sales** page with **Add new lead**, or save a caller from [Call history](/phone/calls).
