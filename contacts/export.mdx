Your data is yours. You can download it any time.

## Export contacts

In **Contacts**, click **Export** to download your contact list as a CSV file.

## Export selected leads

In **Sales**, select the leads you want, then choose **Export CSV**.

## Open the file

Open the CSV in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Each row is one contact.
