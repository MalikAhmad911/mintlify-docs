<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [app.revenuesol.com/sign-up](https://app.revenuesol.com/sign-up), or click **Start free trial** on [revenuesol.com](https://revenuesol.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Enter your email address and follow the prompts, or use one of the sign-in options shown on the page. If you sign up with email, you'll get a verification code to confirm the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start onboarding">
    As soon as your account is created, Revenue Sol takes you to [onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), seven short questions that set up your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the email address you check every day. Trial reminders, billing receipts and new-lead notifications go there.
</Tip>

## Logging in later

Go to [app.revenuesol.com/sign-in](https://app.revenuesol.com/sign-in) and sign in with the same method you used to sign up.

## Looking around first

If you want to see Revenue Sol before signing up, open the [live demo](https://demo.revenuesol.com). It's a sample workspace with example contacts, jobs and conversations. Nothing you do there affects a real account.
