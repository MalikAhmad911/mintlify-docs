Revenue Sol works in any modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You can also install it so it opens like an app, full screen, from your home screen.

## Install on iPhone or iPad

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Safari">
    Go to [app.revenuesol.com](https://app.revenuesol.com) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Tap the Revenue Sol icon on your home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install on Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome">
    Go to [app.revenuesol.com](https://app.revenuesol.com) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install on your computer

In Chrome or Edge, open [app.revenuesol.com](https://app.revenuesol.com) and click the install icon in the address bar.

## Shortcuts

On Android and desktop, long-press (or right-click) the Revenue Sol icon to jump straight to **Inbox**, **Contacts** or **Payments**.

<Note>
  The installed app always shows the latest version of Revenue Sol. There's nothing to update.
</Note>
