The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It shows what needs your attention today and how the business is doing.

## Getting started checklist

New workspaces show a **Getting started** card with five steps. Each one turns green when it's actually done:

| Step | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect a phone number** | A Twilio or RingCentral number is connected in **Settings → Connectors**. |
| **Add your first contact** | You have at least one contact. |
| **Send your first message** | A message has gone out. A text only counts once a phone number is connected; email and social replies count on their own. |
| **Turn on AI Autopilot** | Autopilot is on in **AI Brain**. |
| **Request your first payment** | You've sent a payment request. |

<Warning>
  Start with **Connect a phone number**. Without it, texts from the inbox aren't delivered and the AI receptionist can't answer calls.
</Warning>

You can hide the card with **Dismiss setup guide** once you're set up.

## Cards on the dashboard

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Needs your reply">
    Conversations waiting on you. When everything is answered you'll see **Inbox is all caught up**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Today's schedule">
    Appointments booked for today. Shows **No appointments today** when the day is clear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hot leads">
    Leads most worth following up right now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pipeline snapshot">
    How many leads sit at each stage of your pipeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weekly pulse">
    A quick look at this week's messages and payments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI Autopilot">
    What your AI did today, such as replies sent and follow-ups. If Autopilot is off you'll see **Autopilot is paused**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reviews">
    Your latest reviews, with a **Send review request** shortcut.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Integrations">
    The status of your connected tools, so you can spot anything that needs reconnecting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recent activity">
    The latest customer activity across your workspace.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Quick actions

Use **Quick actions** to start common tasks without leaving the dashboard, such as writing a **New message** or sending a review request.

## Infinite Agent

The **Infinite Agent** box lets you tell Revenue Sol what you want done in plain English, then click **Send command**.

## Trial status

During your trial, a banner at the top of the dashboard shows your trial status. You can dismiss it. See [Start your trial](/getting-started/start-your-trial).
