After [onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), Revenue Sol asks you to **Choose a plan to continue**. Every self-serve plan starts with a 14-day trial.

## How the trial works

- **14 days** on Starter, Professional or Growth.
- **A card is required** to start. You won't be charged if you cancel before day 14.
- **One trial per business.**
- When the trial ends, your plan continues automatically and your card is charged for the plan and billing period you picked.
- We email you 3 days before the trial ends, so it never sneaks up on you.

<Note>
  Enterprise plans are set up with our team and don't include a self-serve trial. [Contact us](mailto:contact@revenuesol.com) to talk about Enterprise.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Want the AI receptionist? It starts on **Professional**. Starter is the right fit if you mainly need a CRM and an inbox.
</Tip>

## Start the trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or annual billing">
    Use the **Monthly** / **Annual** switch at the top of the plan list. Annual shows the discounted monthly rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Select **Starter**, **Professional** or **Growth**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your card">
    Enter your card on the secure checkout page. You'll see the trial end date before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your organization">
    If you're asked to **Name your organization**, enter your business name. This is the name your team sees.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You land on your [dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard) when it's done.

## Using more AI than your plan includes

Each plan includes a monthly amount of AI usage. If you need more, you can buy one-time AI credit packs from **Billing**. Credits from packs never expire.

## Changing or cancelling

You can switch plans or cancel from **Billing** at any time. If you cancel during the trial, you won't be charged.
