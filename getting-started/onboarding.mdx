Onboarding has seven short steps. Your answers personalize your workspace and give your AI Employee what it needs to talk about your business. You can change everything later in **Settings → Business settings**.

## The seven steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Workspace setup">
    Your **first name**, **last name** and **phone number**. Use a real number you can receive texts on: Revenue Sol sends booking confirmations and missed-call recoveries to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business">
    Your **company name**, **company location**, **industry** and **company website**. Company name and industry are required.

    The website is required unless you check that you don't have one yet. If you add it, Revenue Sol reads it so your AI Employee knows your services, hours and service area from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team">
    How many people work in the business (**Just me**, **2–5 people**, **6–15 people** or **15+ people**) and how long you've been in business. This helps Revenue Sol suggest the right workflows for your crew.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revenue">
    Your approximate yearly revenue, or **Prefer not to say**. It's used to size your dashboards and is never shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goals">
    Pick the goal that sounds most like you. Revenue Sol puts the features that help with it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Focus">
    Choose everything you'd like Revenue Sol to handle first: answering inbound calls 24/7, sending professional quotes, getting approvals and deposits, invoicing and online payments, attracting more clients, or improving client organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How you found us">
    Tell us where you heard about Revenue Sol.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Industries you can choose

HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing, Cleaning, Landscaping & Lawn Care, Pest Control, Garage Door, Home Remodeling, Auto Repair, Dental, Medical Clinic, Real Estate, Salon & Spa, Restaurant / Cafe, Law Firm, Marketing Agency, or Other.

## Teaching your AI from your website

After the last step, Revenue Sol shows **Setting up your AI** while it reads your website. This can take a few minutes, depending on the size of your site.

- When it finishes you'll see **Done! Your AI is ready to go.**
- If it can't read your site, your workspace is still saved and you can sync the website later from **AI Employee → Knowledge Hub**.

<Note>
  Onboarding saves as you go. If you close the tab, you'll continue from the same step next time you sign in.
</Note>
