## The sidebar

Everything in Revenue Sol is one click away in the left sidebar.

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Overview, trial status and what needs attention. |
| **Inbox** | Every customer conversation: SMS, calls, email, WhatsApp and Instagram. |
| **Calls** | Call history, recordings and voicemails. |
| **Email** | Connected email accounts and email conversations. |
| **Contacts** | Your customers and leads, with full profiles. |
| **Sales** | Your lead pipeline: new and contacted leads to follow up. |
| **Calendar** | Appointments, availability and scheduling. |
| **Jobs** | Work orders and dispatch: schedule, assign crew, track and invoice. |
| **Quotes** | Estimates customers approve online. |
| **Payments** | Payment requests, invoices and payment status. |
| **Reviews** | Your reviews, review requests and replies. |
| **Campaigns** | SMS and email campaigns. |
| **Automations** | Rules that run follow-ups and reminders for you. |
| **AI Brain** | Autopilot controls, the approval queue and AI logs. |
| **AI Employee** | Your AI's persona, knowledge and Q&A. |
| **Voice Agent** | AI phone receptionist setup and call history. |
| **AI Search** | How visible your business is in AI answer engines. |
| **Analytics** | Reports and performance. |
| **Web Analytics** | Website traffic, visitors and conversions. |
| **Settings** | Business details, connectors, team, canned responses and reports. |

Some pages depend on your plan. If a page isn't included in yours, Revenue Sol shows what it does and which plan unlocks it.

## Search anything with Ctrl K

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows) or **⌘ K** (Mac), or click the search box at the top, to jump to any page or find a contact by name.

## Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.

| Settings page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business settings** | Business profile, website, phone, address and timezone. |
| **Connectors** | Connect Twilio, RingCentral, Stripe, Google, Retell and SendGrid. |
| **Team** | Invite teammates, assign roles and manage access. |
| **Canned responses** | Saved replies for the inbox. |
| **Reports** | Weekly performance summaries and email digests. |
