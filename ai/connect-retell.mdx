Revenue Sol's AI receptionist runs on your own [Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com) account. Retell bills you directly for voice minutes.

<Note>
  The AI receptionist is included on **Professional** and up. You also need a connected [Twilio number](/phone/connect-twilio).
</Note>

## Before you start

1. Create an account at [retellai.com](https://www.retellai.com).
2. In Retell, create an agent for your business. Note its **Agent ID** (it starts with `agent_`).
3. Copy your Retell **API key** from the Retell dashboard.

## Connect Retell to Revenue Sol

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Retell AI">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and open **Retell AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Paste your **Retell API Key** and save. **Integration status** shows when the connection works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your default agent">
    Click **Sync agents to choose**, then pick your **Default Retell agent**. You can also paste **Your Retell Agent ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your default number">
    Click **Sync numbers to choose**, then pick the **Default Retell number** that should be answered by the AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook details into Retell">
    Under **Webhook credentials**, copy the **Webhook URL** into your Retell agent's webhook setting. A **Webhook Secret** is generated for you if you leave it empty. This lets Revenue Sol log every AI call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Route your Twilio number to the AI

Open **AI receptionist setup** to "prepare the selected Twilio number to route toward the default Retell AI agent." Follow the **Setup steps** shown there. If your setup needs it, the page also shows the details for **Manual SIP setup**.
