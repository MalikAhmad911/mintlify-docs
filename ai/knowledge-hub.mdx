The **Knowledge Hub** holds "the authoritative facts every AI reply is grounded in." Open **AI Employee → Knowledge Hub**.

<Tip>
  The more complete your Knowledge Hub, the fewer questions the AI has to pass to you. Check **Completeness** and **AI Memory Coverage** to see what's missing.
</Tip>

## Business profile

Core company facts used across AI replies and workflows:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Business description** | "Family-owned HVAC company serving Austin since 2012." |
| **Services** (one per line, optional price after `\|`) | `AC Repair \| from $129` |
| **Pricing facts** (one per line) | `Service call: $89` |
| **Service areas** (comma separated) | `Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park` |
| **Business hours** (one per line) | `Mon–Fri: 9am–6pm` |
| **FAQs** (blocks of `Q: … / A: …`) | `Q: Do you offer financing? / A: Yes, on installs over $2,000.` |

<Note>
  Only add prices you're happy for the AI to quote. If a price depends on an inspection, say so in the pricing facts.
</Note>

## Website Knowledge Sync

Revenue Sol can read your website so the AI knows your services, pages and wording.

- Start a sync from **Website Knowledge Sync**.
- **Pages crawled** shows how many pages were read.
- **Crawl History** lists recent website imports for your workspace.

Run a sync again after you update your website. Each sync uses AI credits; see [AI credits](/ai/ai-credits).

## Business Memory

**Business Memory** is the saved business facts available to your AI Employee.

## Client Memories

**Client Memories** are what the AI remembers about each contact: what they're **Interested in**, **Key facts**, **Commitments made**, **Open questions**, and their **Last interaction**. This lets the AI pick up a conversation where it left off.

## CRM Context Preview

**CRM Context Preview** shows what your AI knows across the whole CRM, so you can check it before it talks to customers.
