AI actions in Revenue Sol use **AI credits**. Every plan includes a monthly amount, and you can buy more any time.

## Included each month

| Plan | Included AI credits |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 1,000 |
| Professional | 2,000 |
| Growth | 4,000 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

## What uses credits

| AI action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| An AI reply | 1 |
| An Infinite Agent command | 1 |
| Refreshing what the AI remembers | 1 |
| A minute of AI receptionist call time | 2 |
| A website knowledge sync | 25 |

<Note>
  Credits cover the AI itself. Your phone provider (Twilio or RingCentral) and Retell bill you separately for calls, texts and voice minutes.
</Note>

## Buy more credits

Open **Billing** and find **Need more AI credits?** Packs are one-time purchases, and credits from packs never expire.

| Pack | Credits | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boost | 1,000 | $20 |
| Growth | 3,000 | $50 |
| Power | 5,500 | $80 |
