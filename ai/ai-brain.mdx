**AI Brain** is where you decide what your AI does and how much it does on its own. Open it from the sidebar.

## Turn on Autopilot

Autopilot is the master switch. When it's off, the AI doesn't act on its own and your dashboard shows **Autopilot is paused**. Turn it on at the top of **AI Brain**.

## How much should AI do on its own?

Choose one of three modes:

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual review** | AI suggests what to do, but never texts a customer on its own. |
| **Approval queue** | AI drafts customer-facing messages; you approve them before they send. |
| **Auto-send** | AI sends on its own for the features you've turned on. |

<Tip>
  Start with **Approval queue**. Review a few days of drafts in the [approval queue](/ai/approvals), then switch to **Auto-send**.
</Tip>

## Test mode

**Test mode** means "nothing sends to customers yet." Keep it on while you're setting up, and turn it off when you're ready to go live.

## Business hours only

Turn on **Business hours only** to "limit AI actions to your working hours." Outside those hours the AI doesn't act, and that includes missed-call follow-ups.

## Choose what AI can do

Under **What AI can do**, turn each feature on or off individually. See [What the AI can do](/ai/ai-features).

## Fully live checklist

Your AI is fully live when all three are true:

1. Autopilot is on.
2. The mode is **Auto-send**.
3. **Test mode** is off.
