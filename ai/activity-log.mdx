The **AI activity log** in **AI Brain** shows "everything AI has done for your business recently." It refreshes automatically.

## What each entry shows

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Time** | When the AI acted. |
| **Area** | Which feature it was, such as messages, reviews or payments. |
| **Action** | What the AI did. |
| **Status** | What happened, for example whether it was **escalated to you**. |

When the AI hasn't done anything yet you'll see **No AI activity yet**.

## Use the log to tune your AI

- A reply that missed the mark? Add the right answer as a [Q&A pair](/ai/qa-pairs).
- The AI escalating too often? Adjust [escalation rules](/ai/persona#escalation-rules).
- A feature doing too much? Turn it off in [What the AI can do](/ai/ai-features).
