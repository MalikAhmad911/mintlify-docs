In **AI Brain → What AI can do**, turn each of these on or off individually. They only run when [Autopilot](/ai/ai-brain) is on.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto-reply to messages** | Replies to new texts within about a minute. |
| **Send review requests** | Texts customers for a review after they pay. |
| **Payment reminders** | Nudges unpaid invoices after 24 hours. |
| **Follow up cold leads** | Re-engages leads who've gone quiet for a day. |
| **Missed-call follow-up** | Texts back anyone whose call you missed. See [Missed-call text back](/phone/missed-call-text-back). |
| **Weekly summary** | A recap of what AI handled, every Monday. |
| **Reply to reviews** | Writes replies to your Google reviews. Needs Google Business Profile connected. |

<Note>
  Texting features need a connected phone number. See [Phone and texting setup](/phone/overview).
</Note>

## Reply to reviews

If you see **Connect Google Business Profile to unlock this**, connect Google in **Settings → Connectors** first.

## What decides the AI's answers

The AI writes from what it knows about your business in the [Knowledge Hub](/ai/knowledge-hub), your exact answers in [Q&A pairs](/ai/qa-pairs), and the tone and rules in [Persona settings](/ai/persona).
