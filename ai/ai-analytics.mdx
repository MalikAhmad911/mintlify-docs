**AI Employee** shows how your AI is performing.

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | How many conversations the AI handled. |
| **Conversations Over Time** | The trend by day. Shows **More data needed for trend chart.** until there's enough activity. |
| **Resolution Rate** | How often the AI answered without needing you. |
| **Escalation Rate** | How often it handed a conversation to your team. |
| **Conversion Rate** | The share of AI conversations that led to a conversion, such as a booking. |
| **Top Questions** | What customers ask most. |
| **Recent AI Activity** | The latest conversations the AI handled, with their **Outcome**. |

## Improve your numbers

- **High escalation rate?** Fill in your [Knowledge Hub](/ai/knowledge-hub) and add [Q&A pairs](/ai/qa-pairs) for the top questions.
- **Low conversion rate?** Make sure the AI can book: see [Let the AI receptionist book appointments](/ai/ai-booking).
