The **Voice Agent** is your AI receptionist. It answers calls on your business number, answers questions about your business and can book appointments. Open **Voice Agent** in the sidebar.

<Note>
  Connect [Retell AI](/ai/connect-retell) first. The AI receptionist is included on **Professional** and up.
</Note>

## Set up the agent

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent Name** | What the AI calls itself, for example "Reyes HVAC Assistant." |
| **Greeting Script** | The first thing callers hear, for example "Hello! Thanks for calling. How can I help you today?" |
| **Language** | The language the agent speaks. |
| **Persona / Instructions** | How it should behave: what to ask, what to promise, when to take a message. |

Use **Toggle AI Receptionist** to turn it on or off.

## Sync your knowledge

Click **Sync knowledge to Retell** so the agent knows the services, prices, hours and service areas from your [Knowledge Hub](/ai/knowledge-hub). Sync again whenever you change them.

## Make a test call

Start a test call from the Voice Agent page and talk to your AI in your browser. While it's running you'll see **Test call in progress** and the active call panel, where you can **Mute**, **Hold** or **End call**.

## Review AI calls

The Voice Agent page lists every call the AI handled.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Active, Completed, Ended, Missed or Failed. |
| **Summary** | What the call was about. |
| **Sentiment** | How the caller seemed to feel. |
| **Transcript** | The full conversation. |
| **Recording** | The audio. |

At the top you'll see **Total Calls**, **AI Handled**, **Appointments** and **Avg Duration**. Use **Search phone or summary** to find a call.

## Tips for a better receptionist

- Write the greeting the way your best office manager would answer the phone.
- In the instructions, tell it which jobs you do and don't take, and what to say about pricing.
- Let it [book appointments](/ai/ai-booking) so callers leave with a time slot, not a promise.
