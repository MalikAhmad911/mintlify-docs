**Q&A Pairs** are "approved exact answers your AI can reuse." When a customer asks something that matches, the AI uses your answer instead of writing its own.

## Add a Q&A pair

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Q&A">
    Open **AI Employee** and go to **Q&A Pairs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Q&A">
    Enter the **Question** and your **Answer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The AI starts using it in its next replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good Q&A pairs to add first

- Do you charge for estimates?
- What areas do you serve?
- Are you licensed and insured?
- Do you offer emergency or weekend service?
- What payment methods do you take?
- How soon can someone come out?

## Top questions

**Top Questions** shows what customers ask most. If a top question doesn't have a Q&A pair yet, add one. When nothing has been captured yet you'll see **No questions captured yet.**

<Tip>
  If you reject a draft in the [approval queue](/ai/approvals) because the answer was wrong, add the right answer as a Q&A pair so it's right next time.
</Tip>
