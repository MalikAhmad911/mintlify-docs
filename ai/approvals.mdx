When **AI Brain** is set to **Approval queue**, the AI drafts customer-facing messages and waits for you to approve them.

## Review pending approvals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Brain">
    Click **AI Brain** in the sidebar and find **Pending approvals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the draft">
    Each item shows the message the AI wants to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Approve to send it, or reject it. Rejected drafts are never sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When nothing is waiting you'll see **No pending approvals**.

<Note>
  If the queue says **Approval review is off**, your mode is set to **Manual review** or **Auto-send**. Switch to **Approval queue** in **AI Brain** to review drafts.
</Note>

## AI suggestions

**AI suggestions** are actions the AI recommends. Act on a suggestion or dismiss it. When there are none you'll see **No suggestions waiting**.

## Escalations

When the AI can't confidently answer, it can hand the conversation to you. These show as **escalated to you** in the [AI activity log](/ai/activity-log). Set when this happens in [Persona settings](/ai/persona#escalation-rules).
