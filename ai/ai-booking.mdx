Your AI receptionist can check your real availability and book the appointment while the caller is still on the line.

## Add the custom functions to Retell

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Retell AI in Revenue Sol">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors → Retell AI** and find **Custom function URLs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Check availability URL">
    Click **Copy** next to **Check availability**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Book appointment URL">
    Click **Copy** next to **Book appointment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add both to your Retell agent">
    In your Retell dashboard, add each URL as a custom function on the agent you use for your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Now, during a call, the agent can "check real openings and book appointments straight into this CRM's calendar."

## Make sure your calendar is ready

- Set your availability and appointment types in **Calendar**, so the AI only offers times you can actually do.
- Add your services and service areas in the [Knowledge Hub](/ai/knowledge-hub), so the AI books the right job type.

## Check that it works

Make a [test call](/ai/voice-agent#make-a-test-call) and ask to book a visit. The appointment should appear in **Calendar**, and the call shows up in the Voice Agent call list.
