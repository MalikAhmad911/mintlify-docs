Revenue Sol's AI handles the conversations you don't have time for: it replies to new leads, follows up, texts back missed calls, asks for reviews and answers your phone.

<Note>
  AI features are included on **Professional** and up. Starter doesn't include the AI Employee or the AI receptionist.
</Note>

## What the AI can do

| Task | Where you turn it on |
| --- | --- |
| Reply to new texts within about a minute | **AI Brain → Auto-reply to messages** |
| Text back anyone whose call you missed | **AI Brain → Missed-call follow-up** |
| Re-engage leads who've gone quiet for a day | **AI Brain → Follow up cold leads** |
| Ask customers for a review after they pay | **AI Brain → Send review requests** |
| Nudge unpaid invoices after 24 hours | **AI Brain → Payment reminders** |
| Email you a Monday recap of what it handled | **AI Brain → Weekly summary** |
| Reply to your Google reviews | **AI Brain → Reply to reviews** |
| Answer phone calls 24/7 | **Voice Agent** |

## Set up your AI in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Teach it your business">
    Fill in your business profile and sync your website in the [Knowledge Hub](/ai/knowledge-hub).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a persona">
    Set its name, greeting and when to escalate in [Persona settings](/ai/persona).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in Test mode">
    In [AI Brain](/ai/ai-brain), keep **Test mode** on while you set up. Nothing is sent to customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how much it does on its own">
    Start with **Approval queue** so you approve each message, then move to **Auto-send** when you trust it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the AI receptionist">
    [Connect Retell](/ai/connect-retell) and set up your [Voice Agent](/ai/voice-agent) to answer calls.
  </Step>
</Steps>
