Your AI Employee's persona decides how it sounds and when it hands a conversation to you. Open **AI Employee** in the sidebar.

## Create your AI Employee

If you haven't yet, click **Create AI Employee** and give it an **AI Employee name**. Customers see this name, so pick something friendly, such as "Sam from Reyes HVAC." Use the **AI employee active** switch to turn it on or off.

## Personality

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Name** | How your AI introduces itself. |
| **Tone** | How your AI sounds: **Professional**, **Friendly**, **Casual** or **Formal**. |
| **Reply Length** | How much detail in each reply: **Brief**, **Conversational** or **Detailed**. |
| **Golden Rules** | Rules your AI must always follow, up to 5. For example: "Never promise a same-day visit." |
| **Intro Message** | The first message to new contacts (optional). |
| **Away Message** | The reply sent outside business hours (optional). |

When you save, you'll see **Persona saved — AI replies will use this personality now.**

## Greeting, fallback and blocked topics

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Greeting message** | How the AI opens a conversation. |
| **Fallback message** | What the AI says when it doesn't know the answer. |
| **Blocklist Topics** | Topics the AI should never discuss, such as refund disputes or legal advice. |

## Escalation rules

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto escalate** | Auto-escalates conversations the AI can't confidently answer. |
| **Escalate to** | The team member who gets escalated conversations. Add team members in **Settings → Team** first. |
| **Respect business hours** | Only escalates during business hours. |
| **After hours enabled** | Lets the AI answer after hours with limited responses. |

<Tip>
  Most service businesses get the most out of the AI after hours. Turn on **After hours enabled** so evening and weekend leads get an answer right away.
</Tip>
