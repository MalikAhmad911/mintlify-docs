**Recurring invoices** "auto-send an invoice on a schedule — lawn, cleaning, maintenance plans, memberships." Open them from **Payments** (or search **Recurring invoices** with **Ctrl K**).

## Create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New recurring invoice">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    Pick the **Contact**, or enter the **Customer**, **Phone** and **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Enter the **Amount (USD)** and a **Description**, such as "Monthly lawn service." Add a **Deposit (optional)** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    Under **Every**, choose **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**, and pick the date for **First invoice on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Each invoice is sent automatically on schedule. The list shows the **Next invoice** date for each customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you haven't set any up yet you'll see **No recurring invoices yet.**

## Stop one

Use **Delete** on a recurring invoice to stop future invoices. Invoices already sent aren't affected.

<Tip>
  Pair recurring invoices with [recurring jobs](/jobs/recurring-jobs) so the visit and the bill are both on autopilot.
</Tip>
