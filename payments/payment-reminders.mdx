Let Revenue Sol chase unpaid invoices for you.

## Turn it on

In **AI Brain**, switch on **Payment reminders** ("nudges unpaid invoices after 24 hours"). [Autopilot](/ai/ai-brain) must be on too.

## How reminders are sent

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| First reminder | 24 hours after the payment request is sent |
| After that | At most once every 3 days (72 hours) |
| Maximum | 3 automatic reminders per payment request |
| Stops | Once it's paid, or after 30 days |

Reminders go by text, so you need a connected [phone number](/phone/overview).

## Send one yourself

Need to nudge a customer now? Open the payment and resend the link, or text them from the [inbox](/inbox/reply).
