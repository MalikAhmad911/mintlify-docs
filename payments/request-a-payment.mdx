keywords: ["send payment link by text", "text to pay", "request deposit", "collect payment by SMS", "payment request"]
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new payment">
    In **Payments**, click **New payment**. You can also use **Request payment** in a conversation or on a contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    **Search contact**, or enter the **Contact Name**, **Phone Number** and **Email Address**. A phone number is required to send the link by text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Add the **Amount (USD)** and a **Description**, for example "Invoice #1234" or "Water heater install."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a deposit (optional)">
    Use **Deposit (optional)** to "offer the customer a smaller upfront amount," for example 50.00 to book the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and send">
    Click **Create Payment Request**. The customer gets a text with a secure payment link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Texts need a connected [phone number](/phone/overview).
</Note>
