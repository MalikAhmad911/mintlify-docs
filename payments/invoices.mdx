Open a payment in **Payments** and choose **Invoice** to see it as an invoice.

## What's on the invoice

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Bill to** | The customer's details. |
| Line items, **Subtotal** and **Total** | What was charged. |
| **Payment details** | The **Status**, the **Method** (for example **Card (Stripe)**) and the **Transaction**. |
| **Sent Date** and **Paid Date** | When it was sent and paid. |

## Print or save as PDF

Use the print button on the invoice. To save a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** in your browser's print window.
