When a customer taps the payment link, they see a simple page with your business name, what the payment is for and the **Total**.

## Paying

- Tap **Pay** to pay the full amount by card.
- Or choose **Or pay a custom amount** to pay part of it now.
- If they've already paid part, they see **Paid so far**.

The page shows "Secured by Stripe." The customer doesn't need an account or an app.

## After paying

The payment request changes to **Paid** in **Payments**, and the payment appears on the [invoice](/payments/invoices).

If **Send review requests** is on in [AI Brain](/ai/ai-features), the customer gets a review request after they pay.
