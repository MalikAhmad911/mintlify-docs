## Refund a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment">
    In **Payments**, find the paid payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund it">
    Open **Actions** and choose refund. When it succeeds you'll see **Refund processed.** and the status changes to **Refunded**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see **Refund could not be processed.**, try again later or [contact us](mailto:contact@revenuesol.com).

## Cancel an unpaid request

Sent a link by mistake or agreed a different price? Open **Actions** on the payment request and cancel it. The customer's link stops working. Create a new request if you need to.

<Note>
  Who can refund depends on their role on your team.
</Note>
