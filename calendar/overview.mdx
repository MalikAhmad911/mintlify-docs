Open **Calendar** in the sidebar to see your appointments, including those "booked through your public booking pages."

## Views

Switch between **Today** and **This week**, and use **Previous** and **Next** to move around.

## Filters

Use **All statuses** and **All types** to narrow the calendar to one status or one [appointment type](/calendar/appointment-types).

## Appointment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Booked and expected. Reminders are sent for confirmed appointments. |
| **Completed** | Done. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. |
| **No-show** | The customer didn't turn up. |

To change one, open the appointment and use **Update status**: **Confirm**, **Mark complete**, **Mark no-show** or **Cancel**.
