Book a customer yourself when they call, text or walk in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New appointment">
    In **Calendar**, click **New appointment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    Enter a **Title** (for example "Consultation with John"), the **Customer** name, **Phone** and **Email**, and how many **Minutes** it takes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    Use **Notes** for context your team needs. **Internal notes** are private and never shown to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The appointment appears on the calendar and the customer gets [reminders](/calendar/reminders) while it's confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the visit

Open the appointment and use **Update status** to mark it **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**.

<Tip>
  Booking a job that needs a crew and an invoice? Create a [job](/jobs/overview) instead.
</Tip>
