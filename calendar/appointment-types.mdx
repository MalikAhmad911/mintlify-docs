An appointment type is "a reusable category for appointments," such as **Free estimate**, **Service call** or **Maintenance visit**. Customers pick one on your [booking page](/calendar/online-booking).

## Create an appointment type

Open **Calendar → Settings**. If you haven't made any yet you'll see **No appointment types yet**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What customers see, for example "Consultation." |
| **Description** | A short explanation of what's included. |
| **Duration (minutes)** | How long it takes. |
| **Color** | Its color on your calendar. |
| **Location** | **In person**, **Phone**, **Video** or **Custom**. |
| **Location detail (optional)** | An address or a video link, for example "123 Main St." |

Set a type to **Inactive** to hide it from your booking page without deleting it.

## Ideas for service businesses

| Type | Duration | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
