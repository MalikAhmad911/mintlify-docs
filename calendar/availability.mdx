Open **Calendar → Settings** to decide when customers can book you.

## Weekly availability

Set your hours for each day under **Weekly availability**. Mark a day **Closed** if you don't take bookings that day. Choose your **Timezone** so times show correctly to customers.

## Booking rules

| Setting | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slot interval (minutes)** | How often start times are offered. | 30 → 9:00, 9:30, 10:00 |
| **Minimum notice (hours)** | How far ahead customers must book. | 12 → no bookings for the next 12 hours |
| **Booking window (days ahead)** | How far into the future customers can book. | 30 → up to a month ahead |
| **Max bookings per day (0 = unlimited)** | The most bookings you'll take in a day. | 6 |

<Tip>
  Leave yourself travel time. A slot interval that matches your typical job length, plus a sensible daily limit, keeps the AI and the booking page from overbooking you.
</Tip>

These rules apply to your [online booking page](/calendar/online-booking) and to [AI booking](/ai/ai-booking).
