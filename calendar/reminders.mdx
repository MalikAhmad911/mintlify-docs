Revenue Sol automatically texts your customers before each appointment.

## When reminders go out

| Reminder | When |
| --- | --- |
| Day-before reminder | About 24 hours before the appointment |
| Hour-before reminder | About 1 hour before the appointment |

The day-before text reads: "Hi Jane! Reminder: you have an appointment tomorrow at 10:00 AM. Reply CONFIRM or CANCEL."

The hour-before text reads: "Hi Jane! Your appointment starts in 1 hour at 10:00 AM. See you soon!"

<Note>
  When a customer replies CONFIRM, CANCEL or anything else, the reply lands in your **Inbox** like any other message. Update the appointment's status yourself if they cancel.
</Note>

## Requirements

- The appointment status is **Confirmed**. Cancelled, completed and no-show appointments don't get reminders.
- The customer has a phone number.
- Your workspace has a connected [phone number](/phone/overview).

## Fewer no-shows

- Watch your **Inbox** for replies to reminders, and reschedule or cancel right away.
- Mark missed visits **No-show** in the calendar so you can follow up and see the pattern.
