Your **Public booking page** lets "customers book appointments online, 24/7." Turn it on in **Calendar → Settings**.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create appointment types">
    Add the services customers can book. See [Appointment types](/calendar/appointment-types).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability">
    See [Availability and booking rules](/calendar/availability).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the public booking page">
    Switch on **Public booking page** and add an **Intro message (optional)**, which is shown at the top of your booking page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get notified">
    Enter a **Notify staff email (optional)**, such as `staff@yourbusiness.com`, to get an email for each new booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What customers see

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a service">
    They pick an appointment type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a day and time">
    Only open times are shown. If a day is full they see "No times available this day. Try another day."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your details">
    They enter their **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone** and anything you should know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm booking">
    They see **You're booked!** and "A confirmation has been sent."
  </Step>
</Steps>

The booking appears in your **Calendar** as **Confirmed**, and a new customer is added to your contacts as a lead with the source "booking."

## Share it

Put the booking link in your Google Business Profile, Instagram bio, email signature and on your website's "Book now" button.

## Embed it on your website

The booking page can be embedded on your own site in an `iframe`, so customers book without leaving your website. Ask your web designer to embed your booking page link.
