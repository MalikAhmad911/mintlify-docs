## Assign a conversation

Click **Assign agent** and choose a teammate. They see it in their inbox and can pick it up. Add teammates first in **Settings → Team**.

## Add labels

Click **Add label…** to tag a conversation, such as "Quote sent" or "Warranty." Labels come in ten colors, from Red to Slate, so you can scan the list quickly.

## Set the status

Change **Status** to **Open**, **Pending** or **Closed**. See [Conversation status](/inbox/overview#conversation-status).

## Escalated conversations

Conversations the AI hands to your team are marked **Escalated**. Reply to these first. Set when the AI escalates in [Persona settings](/ai/persona#escalation-rules).
