Open **Inbox** in the sidebar. Every conversation with a customer lands here, whichever channel it came from.

## Channels

| Channel | How to set it up |
| --- | --- |
| **SMS and calls** | [Connect Twilio or RingCentral](/phone/overview) |
| **Email** | [Connect an email account](/inbox/connect-email) |
| **Website chat** | [Add the chat widget to your website](/inbox/website-chat-widget) |
| **WhatsApp and Instagram** | [Connect in the social inbox](/inbox/whatsapp-instagram) |

Messages from the website chat widget arrive as text conversations, so you reply by SMS.

## Conversation modes

Each conversation has a mode that decides who replies:

| Mode | Who replies |
| --- | --- |
| **AI** | Your AI Employee replies on its own. You'll see **AI is replying…** and **AI Handled**. |
| **Draft** | The AI writes a suggested reply (**AI reply suggested!**) and you send it. |
| **Human** | You reply yourself. |

AI replies show **How confident the AI was in this reply**, so you can spot ones to check. AI modes need **Professional** or above and [AI Brain](/ai/ai-brain) turned on.

## Conversation status

| Status | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Needs attention. |
| **Pending** | Waiting on the customer or on something else. |
| **Closed** | Done. |

## Find a conversation

Use **Search conversations...** at the top of the list.
