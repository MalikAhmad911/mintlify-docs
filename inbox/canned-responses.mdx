Canned responses are replies you send often, saved so you can insert them in a second.

## Create a canned response

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Canned responses">
    Go to **Settings → Canned responses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title">
    For example, "Thanks reply."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a shortcut">
    For example, `/ty`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a category (optional)">
    Group replies, such as "greeting" or "pricing."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    For example, "Hi {name}, thanks for reaching out!"
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use it in a conversation

In the composer, open **Templates** and pick the reply. It's added to the message box so you can edit it before sending.

<Warning>
  Placeholders like `{name}` aren't filled in automatically. Replace them with the customer's details before you send.
</Warning>

## Replies worth saving

- On my way: "Hi {name}, your technician is on the way and should arrive in about 30 minutes."
- Quote follow-up: "Hi {name}, just checking you got the quote we sent. Happy to answer any questions."
- After the job: "Thanks for choosing us, {name}! If you have a minute, a quick review really helps."

Use **Delete** to remove a reply you no longer need.
