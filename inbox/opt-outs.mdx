Customers can stop your texts at any time by replying with an opt-out word. Revenue Sol honors it automatically.

## Opt-out words

A reply of exactly one of these words opts the contact out of texts:

`STOP` · `STOPALL` · `UNSUBSCRIBE` · `CANCEL` · `END` · `QUIT`

After that, the conversation shows: "This contact texted STOP — they cannot be sent SMS until they opt back in."

## Opting back in

If the customer replies `START`, `YES` or `UNSTOP`, they're opted back in and you can text them again.

## Campaigns

SMS campaign messages automatically end with "Reply STOP to opt out." if you didn't include it yourself.

## Good texting practice

- Only text people who gave you their number or asked you to contact them.
- Never try to work around an opt-out, for example by texting from another number.
- Marketing consent is opt-in: never pre-check a consent box for a customer.
