The **Social Inbox** keeps "WhatsApp and Instagram messages in one place." It's included on every plan. To open it, press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K**) and search for **Social Inbox**.

## Before you start

- **Instagram**: an Instagram professional (business or creator) account linked to a Facebook Page.
- **WhatsApp**: a WhatsApp Business account set up in Meta.
- You need admin access to those accounts in Meta. If you see **Ask your admin to finish the Meta setup first**, ask the person who manages your Meta business account.

## Connect Instagram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect Instagram">
    A Meta window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    Sign in with the Facebook account that manages your Instagram account, choose the account and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it worked">
    New Instagram messages appear in the Social Inbox. Until one arrives you'll see **No messages yet**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect WhatsApp

Click **Connect WhatsApp** and follow the same Meta sign-in to choose your WhatsApp Business account.

## Manual setup (advanced)

If the Meta window doesn't work for your account, use **Manual setup (advanced) — connect with an access token**. You'll need these from your Meta business settings:

| Field | Where it's from |
| --- | --- |
| **Access token** | A Meta System User token is recommended, because it never expires. |
| **Instagram account ID** | Your Instagram professional account's ID (Instagram). |
| **IG username (optional)** | Your Instagram handle (Instagram). |
| **Phone number ID** | Your WhatsApp number's ID (WhatsApp). |
| **WhatsApp Business Account ID** | Your WhatsApp Business account's ID (WhatsApp). |

## Troubleshooting

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Could not open the Meta connect window.** | Allow pop-ups for app.revenuesol.com and try again. |
| **Connection cancelled.** | The Meta window was closed before you finished. Start again. |
| **Could not connect Instagram** | Check that the Instagram account is a professional account linked to a Facebook Page you manage. |
