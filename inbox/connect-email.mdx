Open **Email** in the sidebar and click **Connect an email account**.

## Choose your provider

| Provider | How it connects |
| --- | --- |
| **Gmail (app password)** | Your Gmail address and a Google app password. |
| **Custom IMAP/SMTP** | Any business mailbox, using its IMAP and SMTP settings. |
| **Outlook / Microsoft 365** | Coming soon. |

## Connect Gmail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google app password">
    In your Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an app password. Google shows a 16-character password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the account in Revenue Sol">
    Choose **Gmail (app password)**. Enter your **Email address**, an optional **Display name** (such as "Reyes HVAC"), and the app password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The account starts syncing. Its status changes from **Syncing** to **Synced**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect another mailbox (IMAP/SMTP)

Choose **Custom IMAP/SMTP** and enter the settings from your email host:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **IMAP host** | `imap.yourhost.com` |
| **IMAP port** | Usually `993` |
| **SMTP host** | `smtp.yourhost.com` |
| **SMTP port** | Usually `465` or `587` |

Your email host's help pages list these values.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Synced** | Email is up to date. |
| **Syncing** | New email is being pulled in. |
| **Not synced** | It hasn't synced yet. |
| **Error** | Check the password or settings and reconnect. |
