Open a conversation and click **Contact details** to see who you're talking to.

## What's in the panel

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name**, **Phone**, **Email** | The contact's details. |
| **Lead status** | Where they are in your pipeline. |
| **Tags** | Tags on the contact. Shows **No tags yet.** when there are none. |
| **Card on file** | Whether a card is saved for them. Shows **No card saved** if not. |
| **Linked lead** | The lead record this conversation belongs to. If you see **Link a contact to manage pipeline status.**, link one first. |
| **Timeline** | Recent activity with this contact. |
| **Quick actions** | **Request payment** and **Send review request**. |

## Go to the full record

Click **Open full contact** or **Open lead profile** to see everything about them in **Contacts**.
