<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    In **Inbox**, click **New Conversation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the customer">
    Add their **Name** and **Phone**, and **Email (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a subject">
    Give it a short **Subject**, such as "Pricing question" or "Demo follow-up," so it's easy to find later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your message and send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Texts are only delivered once a phone number is connected. If you see **Connect Twilio before sending SMS**, follow [Phone and texting setup](/phone/overview).
</Warning>

## Phone number format

Enter numbers with the country code, for example `+1 407 555 1234`.

## Can't text this contact?

If you see "This contact texted STOP," they've opted out and can't be texted until they opt back in. See [Opt-outs](/inbox/opt-outs).
