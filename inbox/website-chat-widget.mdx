The website chat widget adds a chat bubble to your site. When a visitor sends a message, it lands in your **Inbox** as a text conversation, and you reply by SMS, so the conversation follows them after they leave your site.

<Note>
  Replies go out by text, so connect a [phone number](/phone/overview) first.
</Note>

## Add the widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your snippet">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and find the **Website** card. Click **Copy Snippet** next to **JS Snippet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your website">
    Paste the snippet into your site's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag, on every page where you want the chat bubble. Most website builders have a "custom code" or "footer code" setting for this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and test">
    Publish your site, open it, and send a test message. It should appear in your **Inbox**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using WordPress or Elementor?

The **Website** card also offers a **WordPress / Elementor plugin**, so you don't have to paste code.

## If the widget stops working

The snippet includes your workspace's **API Key**. If you regenerate the key in the **Website** card, "existing website widgets using the old key may stop working." Copy the new snippet and replace the old one on your site.
