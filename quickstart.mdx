This guide takes you from sign-up to a workspace that answers customers for you. Plan on about 15 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.revenuesol.com/sign-up](https://app.revenuesol.com/sign-up) and sign up. See [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Answer seven short questions about your business. If you have a website, Revenue Sol reads it so your AI Employee already knows your services and service area. See [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan and start your trial">
    Pick Starter, Professional or Growth. Each starts with a 14-day trial. A card is required, and you won't be charged if you cancel before day 14. See [Start your trial](/getting-started/start-your-trial).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a phone number">
    This is the most important step. Texts, calls and the AI receptionist only work once a phone number is connected. Open **Settings → Connectors** and connect Twilio or RingCentral.
    <Warning>
      Until a number is connected, any text you send from the inbox stays in Revenue Sol and isn't delivered.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first contact">
    Open **Contacts** and add a customer by hand, or import a CSV file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first message">
    Open **Inbox**, start a conversation with your contact and send a text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on your AI Employee">
    On Professional and above, open **AI Brain** to turn on Autopilot so the AI can reply to leads, and **AI Employee** to set its persona and knowledge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The **Getting started** card on your dashboard tracks these steps for you. See [Your dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard).
</Tip>
