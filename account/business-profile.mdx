Your business profile is used across messages, invoices and AI responses. Keeping it accurate means customers see the right name, logo and contact details everywhere.

## Edit your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Business Profile">
    Go to **Settings → Business Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Update any of these fields:

    | Field | Used for |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Business Name** | How you appear to customers in messages, quotes and invoices |
    | **Category** | Your type of business, such as plumbing or HVAC |
    | **Phone** | Your main business number |
    | **Email** | Where customers can reach you |
    | **Website** | Your site, such as `https://example.com` |
    | **Address** | Your business address |
    | **Timezone** | Appointment times, reminders and reports |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Click **Upload logo** and choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image up to 5 MB. You'll see **Logo ready. Save changes to publish it.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Set the right **Timezone**. If it's wrong, appointment times, reminders and online booking slots will be off.
</Warning>
