## Your login

Each person has their own login. To update your name, photo, email address or password, click your profile picture and choose **Manage account**. To sign out, click **Log out** in the sidebar.

## The Security tab

Go to **Settings → Security** to check the security of your workspace.

### Account security

**Two-factor authentication**, **Session management** and **Password** show **Managed automatically**. Revenue Sol handles secure sign-in for every login.

### Connection security

Each connected service you use shows **Protected** once it's set up, or **Pending setup** if it isn't yet:

| Item | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls & SMS** | Your phone provider. See [Connect Twilio](/phone/connect-twilio). |
| **Voice AI** | Your AI receptionist. See [Connect Retell](/ai/connect-retell). |
| **Payments** | Online payments from customers. |

## Export your data

Need a copy of your data? Click **Export Data** in **Settings → Security**, then [contact support](mailto:contact@revenuesol.com) and we'll prepare a full export for you.

To download a list of your contacts yourself, see [Export contacts](/contacts/export).

## Delete your workspace

Deleting a workspace is permanent. To protect your data, [contact support](mailto:contact@revenuesol.com) from the email address on your account so we can verify the request.

<Note>
  Just want to stop paying? You can [cancel your subscription](/billing/cancel) instead. Your data isn't deleted when you cancel.
</Note>
