Go to **Settings → Notifications** to choose which alerts your team receives.

## Alerts

Turn each alert on or off:

| Alert | When you're notified |
| --- | --- |
| **New message received** | A customer sends a new SMS or chat message |
| **AI escalation** | The AI hands a conversation to your staff |
| **New review posted** | A new review appears |
| **Payment received** | A payment request is paid |
| **Trial ending** | Before your trial expires |
| **Weekly report** | A weekly performance summary. See [Weekly reports](/analytics/weekly-reports). |

Changes save automatically and you'll see **Notification settings saved**.

## Browser notifications

Turn on **Browser notifications** to get alerts about new messages, missed calls, AI escalations and payments, even when the Revenue Sol tab is closed.

1. Switch on **Browser notifications**.
2. When your browser asks, click **Allow**.

This applies only to the device and browser you're using. Turn it on separately on each computer.

<Note>
  If you see "Blocked in your browser", notifications were denied for this site earlier. Allow notifications for Revenue Sol in your browser's site settings, then try again.
</Note>

## Notification sound

Turn on **Notification sound** to play a soft chime for urgent alerts, such as AI escalations and missed calls. This also applies only to the device you're on.

## On your phone

Install the [mobile app](/getting-started/mobile-app) to get alerts on the go.
