Give your office staff and technicians their own login so everyone works from the same inbox, calendar and jobs. Each teammate signs in with their own account.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Go to **Settings → Team & Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Under **Invite a teammate**, type their email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick **Admin** or **Member**. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Invite**. They get an email with a link to create their login and join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only admins can invite or manage teammates.
</Note>

## Users included in each plan

| Plan | Users |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 1 |
| Professional | Up to 4 |
| Growth | Up to 15 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Pending invitations count toward your limit. If you've reached it, you'll see a message asking you to upgrade before you can invite more people. See [Change your plan](/billing/change-plan).

## Pending invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted appear under **Pending invitations**. To cancel one, click the **×** next to it. This frees up the seat.

## Remove a teammate

In the **Members** list, click the trash icon next to their name and confirm. They lose access to your workspace right away. Their conversations, notes and history stay in the workspace.

<Tip>
  If your plan changes to one with fewer users, you can still remove teammates and cancel invitations to get back under the new limit.
</Tip>
