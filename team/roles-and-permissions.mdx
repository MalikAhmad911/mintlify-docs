Every teammate has one of two roles.

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access: manage settings, billing and the team. |
| **Member** | Standard access to the workspace for day-to-day work. |

## What Members can do

Members handle the daily work:

- Reply to customers in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview).
- Add and update contacts and leads.
- Create and update tasks, quotes and jobs.
- Send review requests.

## What's for admins only

- Workspace settings and connectors.
- Setting up the AI Employee and Voice Agent, automations and campaigns.
- Billing and plan changes.
- Inviting, removing and changing the role of teammates.
- Deleting records, exporting data and issuing refunds.

## Change someone's role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Go to **Settings → Team & Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new role">
    In the **Members** list, use the dropdown next to their name to choose **Admin** or **Member**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change applies right away and you'll see **Role updated**. You can't change your own role.

<Tip>
  Keep at least two admins so someone can always manage the workspace if one person is away.
</Tip>
