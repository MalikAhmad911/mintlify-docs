When you send a quote, the customer gets a link to a page with your business name, the line items, **Subtotal**, **Discount** and **Total**. No account or app is needed.

## Approving

The customer types their full name under **Type your full name to approve** and approves. That typed name is saved as their signature.

As soon as they approve:

- The quote becomes an invoice.
- If you take card payments, they can pay right away.
- They see "We'll be in touch shortly."

## Declining

They can decline the quote and say why. They see "You declined this quote. Thank you for letting us know." The quote shows as **Declined**, and Revenue Sol saves the reason they gave.

## Expired quotes

If the **Valid until** date has passed, the customer sees "This quote has expired. Please contact us for an updated quote." Send a new quote with current prices.

## Knowing when they've looked

The quote changes from **Sent** to **Viewed** the first time the customer opens it. That's a good moment to follow up.
