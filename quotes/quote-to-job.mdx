Once a customer approves, turn the quote into a job so your crew can do the work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quote">
    Find it in **Quotes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create job">
    Revenue Sol creates a job with the quote's customer and line items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule and assign">
    Open the new job, set the schedule and assign your crew. See [Create a job](/jobs/create-a-job).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The full loop

**Quote** → customer **approves** → **invoice** is created → you **Create job** → crew does the work → customer **pays** → [review request](/ai/ai-features) goes out.
