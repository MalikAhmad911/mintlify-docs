Open **Quotes** to "send estimates customers can approve online — approval turns into an invoice." Quotes are included on every plan.

## Quote statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You're still working on it. |
| **Sent** | Sent to the customer. |
| **Viewed** | The customer opened it. |
| **Approved** | The customer approved and signed it. |
| **Converted** | It became an invoice. |
| **Declined** | The customer turned it down. |
| **Expired** | Its **Valid until** date passed before they answered. |

Use **Search quotes, customers…** to find a quote.

## How a quote flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and send">
    Build the quote and send it. See [Create and send a quote](/quotes/create-and-send).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer approves">
    They open the link, review it and approve it by typing their name. See [What customers see](/quotes/customer-approval).
  </Step>
  <Step title="It becomes an invoice">
    Approval turns the quote into an invoice the customer can pay right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the work">
    Turn it into a job in one click. See [Quote to job](/quotes/quote-to-job).
  </Step>
</Steps>
