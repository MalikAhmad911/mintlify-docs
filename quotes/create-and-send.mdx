## Start a quote

In **Quotes**, start a new quote. For your first one you can "start from a template with example line items, import existing quote data, or create a new quote."

## Fill it in

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer name**, **Phone**, **Email** | Who the quote is for. |
| **Link a contact (optional)** | Connect it to an existing contact. |
| **Line items** | Each service or part with a description and price. Use **Add from price book** for saved services. |
| **Discount ($)** | A dollar amount off. |
| **Tax (%)** | Your sales tax rate. |
| **Valid until** | The date the quote expires. |
| **Notes (optional)** | A message shown on the quote. |
| **Terms (optional)** | Payment terms and conditions. |

Revenue Sol shows the **Subtotal**, **Discount** and **Total** as you go.

## Send it

- Click **Send** to send the quote to the customer.
- Or click **Copy link** and share it however you like, for example in a text from the [inbox](/inbox/reply).

<Tip>
  Set a **Valid until** date about two weeks out. It gives customers a reason to decide, and old prices don't linger.
</Tip>

## Import existing quotes

Moving from another tool? Use **Import quote data** to bring your quotes over.
