<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Revenue Sol cost?">
    Starter is $99/month (1 user), Professional is $199/month (up to 4 users), Growth is $399/month (up to 15 users), and Enterprise is custom. Annual billing costs 35% less. See [Plans and free trial](/billing/plans-and-trial).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. Starter, Professional and Growth have a 14-day free trial. A card is required, and you aren't charged if you cancel before day 14. A business can use the trial once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel any time?">
    Yes, there's no lock-in. On a paid plan you keep full access until the end of your billing period. See [Cancel or resume](/billing/cancel).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a new phone number?">
    No. You can connect a number from your own Twilio account or your RingCentral number. See [Phone overview](/phone/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is missed call text back?">
    When you miss a call, Revenue Sol automatically texts the caller so the lead doesn't go cold. See [Missed call text back](/phone/missed-call-text-back).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI answer my phone?">
    Yes. The Voice Agent, an AI receptionist, can answer calls 24/7 once you connect Retell. See [Voice Agent](/ai/voice-agent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the AI reply to customers without me checking?">
    Only if you want it to. In **AI Brain**, choose **Manual review** or **Approval queue** to check AI replies before they're sent, or **Auto-send** to let the AI reply on its own. See [AI Brain and Autopilot](/ai/ai-brain).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are AI credits?">
    AI actions use AI credits. Each plan includes a monthly amount, and you can buy one-time packs that never expire. See [AI credits](/ai/ai-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my team use the same account?">
    Each teammate gets their own login. Invite them as an Admin or Member from **Settings → Team & Permissions**. See [Invite teammates](/team/invite-teammates).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work with QuickBooks?">
    Yes. Connect QuickBooks Online to sync customers, invoices and payments. See [QuickBooks Online](/integrations/quickbooks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a lead form to my website?">
    Yes. Share or embed your Request Service Form, or install the WordPress plugin. See [Request service form](/integrations/request-form).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Revenue Sol on my phone?">
    Revenue Sol works in your phone's browser, and you can install it on your home screen so it opens like an app. See [Use Revenue Sol on your phone](/getting-started/mobile-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I import my existing customers?">
    Yes. Upload a CSV file of your contacts. See [Import contacts from CSV](/contacts/import-csv).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my data out or delete my account?">
    Contact support for a full data export or to delete your workspace. See [Security and data](/account/security-and-data).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email [contact@revenuesol.com](mailto:contact@revenuesol.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
