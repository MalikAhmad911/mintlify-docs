**AI Search** helps you "get found in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and every AI answer engine." It's included on **Growth** and up.

<Note>
  Connect your website first. Until you do, AI Search shows "Connect a website first."
</Note>

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your **AI Readiness Score** and how much of your content is AI-ready. |
| **Questions** | Questions customers ask about businesses like yours. Use **Find questions** to build an AI-search FAQ. |
| **Content** | Analyze your pages and create AI-ready content. |
| **llms.txt** | Generate and publish an `llms.txt` file that tells AI engines about your business. |
| **Issues** | Problems to fix. **Mark fixed** when done. |
| **Engines** and **Citations** | Engine visibility monitoring is coming soon. |

## Publish llms.txt

Choose how to publish it:

| Option | How |
| --- | --- |
| **WordPress plugin** (easiest) | **Download the plugin**, install it in WordPress, and it "auto-publishes llms.txt, FAQ schema & AI-crawler access." |
| **Revenue Sol hosted** | "We host your file. Add a redirect from yourdomain.com/llms.txt to the hosted URL." |
| **Manual upload** | "Download the file and upload it to your site root, then test yourdomain.com/llms.txt." |

It "works with WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and any website."

## Other settings

- **Allow AI crawlers** so AI engines can read your site.
- **Inject FAQ schema** to add your FAQ answers to your pages in a format AI engines understand.

<Tip>
  Add your business name, location and phone in **Settings → Business settings**. AI Search uses them as your official business details.
</Tip>
