Open **Analytics** to "track performance across conversations, reviews, payments, campaigns, and AI activity." Pick a date range and click **Apply Range**.

## Headline numbers

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Messages sent** / **Messages received** | Texts and messages in and out. |
| **Calls** | Calls in the period. |
| **Revenue collected** | Payments received. |
| **Reviews collected** / **Avg rating** | New reviews and their average. |
| **AI resolution** | How often the AI resolved a conversation itself. |

## Charts

| Chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations over time** | Inbound and outbound message volume. |
| **Revenue Trend** | Paid payment requests over time. |
| **Review Ratings** | A breakdown by star rating. |
| **Campaign Delivery** | Sent, delivered, replied and failed. |
| **AI Performance** | Resolved, escalated, converted and failed. |

## Top lists

- **Top Contacted Leads**: contacts with the highest engagement.
- **Top Performing Campaigns**: ranked by reply rate and delivery.
