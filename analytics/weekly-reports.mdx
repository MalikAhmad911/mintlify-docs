## Weekly email digest

Open **Settings → Reports** to see "your last 7 days at a glance, plus a weekly email digest for your team." Turn on **Weekly email digest** to get it by email.

<Note>
  Only admins can change digest settings.
</Note>

## Weekly AI summary

On Professional and up, turn on **Weekly summary** in **AI Brain** to get "a recap of what AI handled, every Monday." See [What the AI can do](/ai/ai-features).
