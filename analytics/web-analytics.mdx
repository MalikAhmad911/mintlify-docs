**Web Analytics** shows your website's traffic and search performance next to your leads. It's included on **Growth** and up.

## Connect Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Web Analytics">
    Click **Web Analytics** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with Google">
    Click **Connect with Google** and sign in with the Google account that has access to your Search Console and Analytics 4 properties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    You'll see **Google is connected** when it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Data syncs automatically every day.

## What you'll see

| Source | Numbers |
| --- | --- |
| **Search Console** | **Clicks**, **Impressions**, **Avg CTR** and **Avg Position**, plus top queries, pages, devices and countries. |
| **Analytics 4** | Users, **New Users**, sessions, **Avg Session**, **Bounce Rate**, traffic sources, **Conversions** and **Conv. Rate**. |

If a range has no data yet you'll see "No Search Console data for this range yet." or "No Analytics data for this range yet."

## Ask Google to index a page

Published a new page? Enter its URL and click **Request indexing**. You'll see "Submitted to Google for indexing."
