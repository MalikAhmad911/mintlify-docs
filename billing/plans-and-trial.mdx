Go to **Billing** (or **Settings → Billing**) to see your plan, AI credits and payment history in one place.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Users | AI credits / month | Phone numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | $99 | $64 | 1 | 1,000 | 1 |
| **Professional** | $199 | $129 | Up to 4 | 2,000 | Up to 5 |
| **Growth** | $399 | $259 | Up to 15 | 4,000 | Up to 16 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing costs 35% less than paying monthly and is charged once a year. Every plan has unlimited contacts. Click **Compare plans** on the Billing page to see the features in each plan, or visit [revenuesol.com/pricing](https://revenuesol.com/pricing).

For Enterprise, click **Let's talk** and our team will put together a plan for you.

## How the free trial works

Starter, Professional and Growth start with a **14-day free trial**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose a plan and **Monthly** or annual billing, then click **Start 14-day free trial**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your card">
    Enter your card details. There's no charge today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try everything">
    Use your plan's features for 14 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it or cancel">
    If you do nothing, your plan starts and your card is charged when the trial ends. Cancel before day 14 and you won't be charged at all. See [Cancel your subscription](/billing/cancel).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A business can use the free trial once. If your workspace or card has already had a trial, you'll see a message saying so, and your plan is billed when you check out.
</Note>

## AI credits

Each plan includes a monthly amount of AI credits. Need more? Buy a one-time pack under **Need more AI credits?** on the Billing page. Pack credits never expire. See [AI credits](/ai/ai-credits).
