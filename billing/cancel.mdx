There's no lock-in. You can cancel whenever you like.

## Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Billing** and find **Cancel subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start cancelling">
    Click **Cancel subscription**. You'll see what you'll lose and some options, such as switching to a smaller plan or talking to us first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Select a reason and, if you like, tell us what we could have done better.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm to cancel. You'll see **Cancellation confirmed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you cancel

- **During your free trial**: cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged at all.
- **On a paid plan**: you keep full access until the end of your current billing period, and nothing more is billed.

When the period ends, your workspace is locked until you choose a plan again. To unlock it, go to **Billing** and pick a plan.

<Tip>
  Paying for more than you use? [Switch to a smaller plan](/billing/change-plan) instead and keep the core features at a lower price.
</Tip>

## Changed your mind?

Until your plan ends, the Billing page shows **Subscription ending** with the date your access ends. Click **Resume subscription** and your plan continues as before.

## Payment failed?

If a renewal payment fails, your plan shows **Past due**. You keep access while the payment is retried. To fix it, [update your payment method](/billing/payment-method-and-history).
