## Update your card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Billing** and find **Payment method & invoices**. It shows the card on file, such as **Visa •••• 4242**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update it">
    Click **Update payment method**. A secure page opens where you can change your card. It takes less than a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Card details are stored securely by Stripe, our payment processor. Revenue Sol never sees your full card number.

## Billing history

**Billing history** on the Billing page lists your billing activity, such as payments, plan changes and cancellations, with the date and amount of each. Where an invoice is available, click **Invoice** to open it.

If you haven't paid anything yet, you'll see **No payments yet — your history will appear here.**

## Paid in another tab?

If you finished checkout in another tab or window and the Billing page hasn't caught up, click **Refresh status** under **Already paid in another tab?** You'll see **Billing updated — payment confirmed.** or **Billing is up to date.**
