Only admins can change the plan. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions).

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Under **Plans**, pick monthly or annual billing, then click **Upgrade to …** or **Downgrade to …** on the plan you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change">
    A summary shows what you're getting, or what you'll lose access to, and when you'll be charged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm upgrade** or **Confirm downgrade**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you're charged

| Change | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Upgrade** | Your saved card is charged the prorated difference today. The new rate applies from now on. |
| **Downgrade** | The change takes effect at your next renewal. Your data stays safe, and everything is restored if you upgrade again. |
| **Any change during your free trial** | No charge today. You're charged the new plan's price when your trial ends, and the summary shows the exact date and amount. |

## Before you downgrade

A smaller plan can include fewer users, AI credits or phone numbers:

- If you have more teammates than the new plan allows, remove some or cancel pending invitations. See [Invite teammates](/team/invite-teammates).
- Features that aren't in the new plan are listed under **You'll lose access to** before you confirm.

<Tip>
  Switching from monthly to annual billing saves 35%. See [Plans and free trial](/billing/plans-and-trial).
</Tip>
