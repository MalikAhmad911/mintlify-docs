Not sure which message works better? Send two versions and let the replies decide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your first message">
    Create a campaign as usual. See [SMS campaigns](/marketing/sms-campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Variant B">
    Click **Add Variant B for A/B test** and write the second version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The audience is split 50/50 between the two messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare results">
    Open the campaign and compare **Delivered** and **Replies** for each version. See [Campaign results](/marketing/campaign-results).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to test

- **The offer**: "$25 off" against "free filter with every tune-up."
- **The call to action**: "Reply YES" against "Book here."
- **The opening**: the customer's first name against your business name.

Change one thing at a time so you know what made the difference.
