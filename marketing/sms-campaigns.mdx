**Campaigns** let you "send updates, offers, reminders, and follow-ups to your customer list" by text. Campaigns are included on **Professional** and up.

<Note>
  Campaigns are sent by SMS, so you need a connected [phone number](/phone/overview) and [A2P 10DLC registration](/phone/a2p-10dlc) for US numbers.
</Note>

## Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Campaign">
    In **Campaigns**, click **Create Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Campaign name**, such as "Spring HVAC Tune-Up Offer." Customers don't see this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your audience">
    Send to all contacts, to contacts with certain tags, or to contacts at certain stages. The **Audience preview** shows who will get it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Type your **Message** and check the **SMS preview**. To test two versions, see [A/B testing](/marketing/ab-testing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Choose **Queue immediately**, or pick a future date and time. Times use your business profile's timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Review the summary and send. You'll see **Campaign scheduled.** for a future send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compliance is built in

- "Opted-out contacts and contacts without phone numbers are automatically excluded."
- An opt-out footer is added automatically, for example "Reply STOP to opt out." See [Opt-outs](/inbox/opt-outs).

## Campaign ideas for service businesses

| Campaign | Audience | Example message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal tune-up | Stage: Customer | "Hi! It's time for your spring AC tune-up. Reply YES and we'll book you in." |
| Win back | Stage: Lost | "We'd love another chance. Here's $25 off your next visit this month." |
| Membership | Tag: Maintenance plan | "Your next maintenance visit is due. Reply with a day that works." |
