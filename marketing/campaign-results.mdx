## Across all campaigns

| Number | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total sent** | Messages sent across all your campaigns. |
| **Avg delivery rate** | How many messages reached phones. |
| **Avg reply rate** | How many customers replied. |

## For each campaign

Open a campaign with **View** to see its "preview and delivery summary":

| Number | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Messages sent. |
| **Delivered** | Messages that reached the phone. |
| **Replies** | Customers who replied. Replies land in your [Inbox](/inbox/overview). |
| **Opted out** | Customers who replied STOP to this campaign. |

## Reuse a campaign

Click **Duplicate** to start a new campaign from one that worked. Use **Delete** to remove an old one.

## Reading the numbers

- **Low delivery rate?** Check your [A2P 10DLC registration](/phone/a2p-10dlc). Unregistered numbers get filtered.
- **High opt-outs?** You may be texting too often, or the offer isn't relevant to that group.
- **Lots of replies?** Answer fast, or turn on [Auto-reply to messages](/ai/ai-features) so the AI does.
