Open **Reviews** for your "review inbox, requests, replies, and settings." Reviews are included on **Professional** and up.

## Your numbers

| Number | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Average rating** | Your average star rating. |
| **Total reviews** | How many reviews you have. |
| **Response rate** | How many reviews you've replied to. |
| **This month** | Reviews received this month. |

## Analytics

- **Rating Trend**: your average rating over time.
- **Review Volume**: reviews received by period.
- **Sentiment**: positive, neutral and negative reviews.

Change the period with **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **Last 90 Days** or **All Time**. Until you have enough reviews you'll see **Not enough review data yet**.
