---
title: "Show your reviews on your website"
sidebarTitle: "Reviews widget"
description: "Add Revenue Sol's reviews widget to your website with one snippet so visitors see your latest customer reviews and star rating."
keywords: ["reviews widget for website", "embed Google reviews", "testimonials widget", "show reviews on website"]
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the snippet">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors**, find the **Website** card and click **Copy Reviews Snippet** under **Reviews widget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it on your site">
    Paste it where you want the reviews to appear, such as your home page or a "Reviews" page. Most website builders have an "embed" or "custom HTML" block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the page and check the reviews show up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Using WordPress or Elementor? The **Website** card also has a **WordPress / Elementor plugin**.
</Tip>

The widget uses your workspace's API key. If you regenerate it in the **Website** card, copy the new snippet onto your site.
