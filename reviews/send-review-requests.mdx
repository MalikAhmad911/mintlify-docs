## Send one review request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Send Review Request">
    In **Reviews**, click **Send Review Request**. You can also use **Send review request** on a contact or in a conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the contact">
    Choose who to ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the platform">
    Under **Platform preference**, choose **Google**, **Facebook** or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the message">
    Check the **Message preview** and edit it. The default reads: "Hi there, thank you for choosing us. We'd appreciate your review when you have a moment."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    You'll see **Review request sent.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Paste your Google review link into the message so customers can leave the review in one tap. To get it, open your Google Business Profile and copy the "Ask for reviews" link.
</Warning>

## Send to many customers at once

On the **Sales** page, select the leads you want and choose **Review request**. Each customer gets a personal text such as "Hi Maria, thanks for choosing Reyes HVAC! We'd really appreciate a quick review."

<Note>
  Bulk and automatic requests use this standard message. To include your Google review link, send requests one at a time and paste the link into the message.
</Note>

## The best time to ask

Right after the job, while the customer is happy. A text the same day gets far more replies than an email weeks later.
