"Reply faster with AI-crafted responses." Every synced Google review in **Reviews** comes with an AI reply suggestion.

## Write a reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Find it in **Reviews**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start from the AI suggestion">
    Use the suggested reply, or type your own in **Write your public reply…**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Revenue Sol saves your reply and marks the review as replied, which counts toward your **Response rate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post it on Google">
    Copy the reply and post it on the review in your Google Business Profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Revenue Sol doesn't publish replies to Google for you yet. The reply only appears on Google once you post it there.
</Note>

## AI review replies in AI Brain

**Reply to reviews** in **AI Brain** writes replies for new reviews so they're ready when you open **Reviews**. It needs Google Business Profile connected: if you see **Connect Google Business Profile to unlock this**, [connect Google](/reviews/connect-google) first.

## Replying well

- Thank the customer by name and mention the job.
- For a bad review, apologize, don't argue, and offer to make it right offline.
- Reply within a day or two.
