<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reviews">
    Click **Reviews** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google">
    Click **Connect Google Business Profile** and sign in with the Google account that manages your business listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on review sync">
    Turn on **Enable Google review sync**. You'll see **Google Business connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How syncing works

- Reviews sync automatically every 6 hours. Before the first sync you'll see "Not synced yet · syncs automatically every 6 hours."
- Click **Sync reviews now** to pull new reviews right away.
- When everything's current you'll see "Google reviews are up to date."

New Google reviews "appear here automatically," with AI reply suggestions under each one. See [Reply with AI](/reviews/reply-with-ai).

<Note>
  You need to be an owner or manager of the Google Business Profile to connect it.
</Note>
