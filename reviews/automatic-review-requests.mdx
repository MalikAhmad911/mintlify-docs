Let Revenue Sol ask every paying customer for a review.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Brain">
    Click **AI Brain** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Send review requests">
    It "texts customers for a review after they pay."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure Autopilot is on">
    Automatic requests only go out while [Autopilot](/ai/ai-brain) is on and not in **Test mode**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

- Professional plan or above.
- A connected [phone number](/phone/overview), because requests go by text.
- The customer hasn't opted out of texts.

<Warning>
  Automatic requests use a standard message that doesn't include your Google review link yet. Customers can still find you on Google, but a link makes reviewing much easier. For important customers, send the request yourself with the link pasted in. See [Send review requests](/reviews/send-review-requests).
</Warning>

<Note>
  If Autopilot is in **Approval queue** mode, each request waits for your approval in **AI Brain** before it's sent.
</Note>
